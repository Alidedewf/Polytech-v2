About

We are building the future of technology today

PolyTech Park is a technology park at Satbayev University, where science meets industry. For over 90 years the university has developed engineering sciences in close connection with the real economy, and the technopark brings research into practice — from work in its own laboratories to deploying solutions in the construction and industrial sectors.
